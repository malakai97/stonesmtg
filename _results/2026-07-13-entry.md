The gang was feeling so excited about Middle School that we decided to immediately follow the School's Out league with Summer School! This was our first Summer School session [since all the way back in 2022](https://stonesmtg.com/results/2022-08-12/), and it was great to be back.

![]({{site.cdn_url}}/assets/images/2026-07-13/summer_school_prizes.jpg)

We couldn't get it together on a Summer School stamp, but we did get to use our new Chaos Orb Burger stamp! In addition to stamped Prohibits as our participation cards, Team Serioso Chad Proctor-Frazier donated these lands as our Top 8 prizes. Thanks Chad - what a chad move!

We had 36 players, which felt really healthy coming right on the heels of another league. Kai Schafroth, who's been playing with us  forever and this year took over Austin Vintage TO duties, went on an amazing undefeated run to win the league. Congrats, Kai, and welcome to the Invitational!

And guess what: we've still got the Middle School bug! Stay tuned for Fall Semester info . . .
